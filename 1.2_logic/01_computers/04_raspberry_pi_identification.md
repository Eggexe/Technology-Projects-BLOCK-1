SOC - The name given to the single microschip that combines a CPU, GPU and some I/O processes onto one piece of silicon. System on chip
GPIO - General purpose I/O. Bank of pins on the edge of the pi acting as an interface between the pi and other hardware. 
USB - Universal serial bus, a standardised method for interaction between external hardware and peripherals.
HDMI - High definition multimedia interface - physical connector for digital video to display on a monitor
Power - power connector to provide 3.3V/5V power to the pi.
microSD - acts as permanent storage for the pi
Network - Physical port for the ethernet cable
Wireless - enables wireless support for Wi-Fi connection for the pi

## Reflection
Why are many components integrated into a single board in embedded computers such as the Raspberry Pi?
Write **100 words**.
Raspberry pis are required to have many components embedded into it as pi's are frequently used for a majority of tasks, ranging from general tinkering to niche uses such as homelabbing setups or other uses. The vast range of components increases the versatility of the pi in these diverse tasks.
